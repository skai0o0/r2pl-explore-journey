## Stage-specific approvals

**M1 architecture gate:** owner accepted the M0 baseline, ADR-003 world representation and M1's ADR-004 chunk/sector mapping. M1 uses a generic immutable registry while the ADR-009 production 0–7 mapping remains proposed; 8–15 stay reserved for production. ADR-011 is open for M3. Exact HQ coordinates, exploration balance, gacha and external integration are **not** M1 gates.

**M2:** the owner authorized a deny-by-default synthetic visibility fixture and ephemeral, nonrewarding commands; C-08 remains open for production. **M3:** benchmark and choose ADR-011 while enforcing atomic economy/world mutation outcomes. **M4:** resolve C-01–05, C-08–09 and C-12 for the playable slice. **M5:** resolve C-11 with the external team. **M6:** resolve C-06–07, C-10, C-13 and remaining content/operations details before rewards go live.
